# Welcome

This is my personal knowledge base for sharing practical notes on digital hardware design and high-speed networking.

Topics I am exploring and documenting include:

- **Digital design**: RTL architecture, verification, timing, and implementation tradeoffs
- **FPGA**: prototyping, tool flows, debugging, and system integration
- **ASIC**: design methodology, synthesis, physical design concepts, and silicon-oriented thinking
- **Ethernet**: protocols, MAC/PHY design, packet processing, and high-speed interfaces

The goal is to turn lessons learned from study and hands-on work into clear, reusable references.
